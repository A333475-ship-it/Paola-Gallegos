## Brand & Style

This design system channels the rigorous discipline of Swiss International Typographic Style fused with modern, high-density executive analytics. Engineered for corporate boards, wealth managers, and institutional analysts, the visual narrative prioritizes objective legibility, mathematical balance, and confident understatement.

The emotional core balances clinical precision with calculated prestige. The experience feels fastidious, resolute, and authoritative. The design style relies on **Minimalism with Precision Structuralism**: strict horizontal/vertical alignment rules, fine hairline boundaries, deliberate white space, tabular clarity, and surgical crimson rose accents that direct executive attention exclusively to significant deltas, critical alerts, and actionable milestones.

## Colors

The palette establishes an executive hierarchy rooted in high-contrast optical clarity:

- **Primary (`#D93856`)**: Swiss Rose / Crimson. Reserved strictly for primary focal triggers, pivotal key performance indicators (KPIs), active playback states, critical trends, and active selection anchors.
- **Secondary (`#334155`)**: Slate Navy. Establishes structural components, section titles, primary interactive axes, and non-critical data visualization marks.
- **Tertiary (`#E05370`)**: Rose Vibrance. Used for interactive hover states, micro-interactions, secondary chart series, and positive metric indicators requiring high-frequency nuance.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`)**: Deep Precision Obsidian. Deployed for root text, high-density figures, tabular values, and structural anchors.

### Named Accent Colors
- `rose-tint` (`#FCE8EC`) and `rose-muted` (`#F5A3B3`) supply soft contextual backing for active timeline scrubbers, tagged delta pills, and regional SVG heatmaps.
- `alpine-white` (`#FFFFFF`), `alpine-subtle` (`#F8FAFC`), and `alpine-canvas` (`#F1F5F9`) define the crisp, high-altitude background layers.
- `precision-slate` (`#64748B`), `precision-muted` (`#94A3B8`), and `precision-border` (`#E2E8F0`) provide the structural hairline grid, inactive axes, labels, and metadata markers.

## Typography

The typography is built upon the neutral, hyper-legible architecture of **Inter**, echoing Swiss modernist objectivity. Strict guidelines govern data density:

- **Tabular Figures (`tnum`)**: All financial tables, numeric telemetry, axis tick marks, and timeline timestamps must enforce OpenType `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` to guarantee vertical column alignment across rapidly updating state figures.
- **Labels**: Micro-labels, table column headers, and data metadata use `label-md` and `label-sm` with upper-case styling and deliberate letter-spacing (`0.04em`–`0.06em`) for immediate scannability.
- **Hierarchy Disciplines**: Never mix more than two weights within a single component. Contrast is established through size, opacity variations of slate greys, and precise spacing rather than decorative typographic treatments.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout adheres to a disciplined 12-column grid engine optimized for widescreen analytic dashboards:

- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12-column grid with `margin-lg` (2.5rem / 40px) outer padding and `gutter-md` (1.5rem / 24px) gutters. Complex dashboards split cleanly into modular layouts (e.g., 8-column canvas with a 4-column drill-down inspector; or 3-col equal KPI cards).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column grid with `margin` (1.5rem / 24px) margins and `gutter` (1rem / 16px) gutters. Secondary inspector panels collapse into swipeable slide-overs or stacked sections.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid with `margin-sm` (1rem / 16px) canvas boundaries. KPI rows convert into horizontal scroll carousels or stacked 2-column cards; data tables transition to card-based summary rows.

Spacing follows an 8pt architectural rhythm with intermediate 4pt tokens (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) enabling dense tabular layouts and compact dashboard widgets.

## Elevation & Depth

In alignment with Swiss minimalist principles, depth is constructed via **tonal layering and low-contrast hairline outlines** rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Base Canvas**: The global background uses `alpine-canvas` (`#F1F5F9`) or `alpine-subtle` (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Surface Elevation (Cards & Panels)**: Elevated content cards sit on pure `alpine-white` (`#FFFFFF`), bounded by a crisp 1px hairline border of `precision-border` (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Subtle Ambient Depth**: Elevated menus, tooltips, and floating timeline scrubber handles use an ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Active Focus & Modals**: Overlay sheets use an ultra-thin border with an elevated shadow: `box-shadow: 0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft (Level 1)** geometric standard (0.25rem / 4px base radius):

- **Data Cards, Chart Containers & Modals**: Built with `rounded-md` (4px to 6px) boundaries to retain technical, engineered sharpness without aggressive brutalism.
- **Filter Chips, Badges & Toggles**: Use refined 4px roundedness or fully bounded capsules (for status pills) to delineate interactive controls from display canvases.
- **Interactive Scrubber Handles & Nodes**: Circular geometries (100% radius) reserved exclusively for data point anchors, status indicators, and timeline play-axis drag pins.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Swiss Rose (`#D93856`) fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Flat finish, 4px border radius, 36px standard executive height, `font-weight: 600`. Hover state transitions to `#E05370`.
- **Secondary**: `#FFFFFF` background with 1px `precision-border` outline, text in `secondary` (`#334155`). Hover transitions to `#F8FAFC` background with text in `neutral` (`#0F172A`).
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Zero border, transparent fill, text in `#64748B`. Hover produces a subtle `#F1F5F9` background tint.

### Data Tables (High Density)
- **Header**: Height 36px, `alpine-subtle` background, text in `label-sm` upper-case with `#64748B` color. Bounded by a 1px solid bottom border (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Rows**: Fixed 40px height for dense executive viewing. Alternating subtle zebra striping optional; hover row background tints to `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.8)`.
- **Numerical Data**: Right-aligned, formatted with `tabular-data` styling (`tnum`), positive deltas flagged with `#334155` and negative deltas marked with `#D93856` tag indicators.

### Interactive Filter Chips
- **Default**: Pill/soft-rectangle with `alpine-white` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `#334155` label, and optional cross icon.
- **Selected**: Inset tinted background of `rose-tint` (`#FCE8EC`), border colored in `#D93856`, text styled in `#D93856` at `font-weight: 600`.

### Timeline / Play-Axis Scrubber
- **Rail**: 4px track styled in `precision-border` (`#E2E8F0`), with played segments painted in `#D93856`.
- **Thumb Handle**: 14px circular disc in `#FFFFFF` with a 2px `#D93856` ring and a delicate ambient shadow.
- **Temporal Controls**: Play/pause square-icon button (28px × 28px) flanked by micro timestamp badges rendered in tabular numbers.

### Regional Switzerland SVG Maps & Chart Canvases
- **Map Vector styling**: Cantonal borders delineated by 0.75px stroke in `#FFFFFF` or `#E2E8F0`. Non-active cantons filled with `#F1F5F9`; data-density choropleth scales smoothly interpolate from `rose-tint` (`#FCE8EC`) through `rose-muted` (`#F5A3B3`) up to `#D93856`.
- **Hover/Selected Canton**: 1.5px stroke in `#0F172A`, floating precision tooltip displaying canton name, current metric, and delta percentage.
- **Chart Frames**: Grid lines must be hairline dashed lines (`#E2E8F0`). Zero baseline rendered solid (`#94A3B8`). Tooltip overlays utilize pure white backgrounds with 1px `#E2E8F0` borders and tabular metric summaries.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields**: 36px height, `alpine-white` background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `body-md` typography. Focus state replaces border with 1px `#D93856` alongside an optical 2px focus ring tinted in `rgba(217, 56, 86, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 16px × 16px square/circle with a 1px border. Checked state displays solid `#D93856` fill with crisp white vector glyphs.
